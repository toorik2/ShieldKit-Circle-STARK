# V17 argument and evidence

V17's argument is not “a large transaction passed.” It is that one fixed
private relation, one sealed proof system, one canonical proof, one graph-owned
verifier bank, value-neutral auxiliary infrastructure, and one state- and
value-changing settlement role are bound to the same exact identity and then
replayed through independent boundaries.

## 1. Constitutional relation

One transition is exactly deposit, full withdrawal, or withdrawal with one
same-owner private change note. The construction contains no transfer, merge,
split, batching, administration, emergency exit, protocol fee, or trusted
verification route.

The relation construction descriptor remains frozen at version 16 and is
byte-identical to the V16 relation program. V17 is proof version 17 and product
graph version 17. This separation is intentional: changing packaging or the
proof theorem does not silently change what is proven.

The private relation enters consensus through one eight-word SHA-256 statement
digest. CashVM independently reconstructs the public statement, while private
note data, ownership, amount, membership path, and change allocation stay on
the sealed side of that boundary.

There is no serialized composition-partial bridge and no second note-aware
opening path. AIR algebra and the whole quotient are checked once at the QM31

to FRI layer zero, and 44 FRI roles verify every fold and the final polynomial.

Five binary matrix trees and nine graph-fixed quartet-first/binary FRI trees
share one strict Merkle language. The proof chooses no arity, domain label,
cut, rank, index, or merge rule.

## 3. BCH-native purification

V17's packaging mechanisms are deliberately narrow:

- the typed graph is the single source for C1-C33 ownership and all 210 roles;
- the q44 query family uses one generic body at fixed ordinals, not 44 unrolled
  programs and not a proof-controlled interpreter;
- one affine reader derives every carrier interval from proof length;
- allocation uses a monotone, hashed coordinatewise closure with an explicit
  no-change terminal replay;
- ROM planning begins with an exact length-only, non-executable census and may
  promote a body only after exact all-profile BCHN measurement; and
- function IDs are stable minimal positive occurrence-anchor encodings, not
  ordinals of an unstable body-class set.

The linked result uses one authenticated ROM page. Its 13 bodies are exact-byte
duplicates, and the page is strictly byte-saving in every profile. The page and
all proof workers are recreated at the same position, lock, value, token state,
sequence, and payload; verifier infrastructure cannot receive or subsidize
settlement value.

## 4. Static theorem, runtime promotion

The source theorem has 16 assurance rows. Eleven mathematical rows are cited or
proved without an artifact. The five product rows deliberately remain
`unresolved` in the static export because no future proof or transaction may be
qualified from source intent alone.

The runtime path promotes only those five rows after binding exact fresh
evidence for:

1. first-reduction transcript correspondence;
2. the singleton OOD/AIR role;
3. all 44 batch-link roles;
4. the 17-challenge grouped-FRI transcript; and
5. the strict mixed-Merkle codec and all final Merkle roles.

It then re-evaluates all 16 rows and 14 randomized rounds. For the exact receipt
identity, the certified classical claim is:

```text
epsilon(T) / T <= 2^-100 for every integer 1 <= T < 2^128.
```

This is a classical programmable-ROM transcript and soundness result under the
cited S-two formulas, the construction-specific L1-L9 correspondences, and
SHA-256 assumptions. It is not a computational-ZK or QROM-ZK result.

## 5. Privacy argument

Each protected M31 column is committed only as `w + Z_H r`, with an independent
uniform `2^18`-coefficient mask. The whole quotient exposes no decomposition.
One independent degree-below-`2^20` QM31 mask enters the FRI batch with
coefficient exactly one.

The complete transaction observer strictly reconstructs every carrier slice,
replays the transcript and all opening schedules, and evaluates the actual
algebraic view. Each protected original column has 48 observed M31 equations
against 262,144 trace and 262,144 mask coefficients. The observer constructs a
constant-one trace delta and 48-coefficient compensating mask with zero
residual, proving that this opening view does not uniquely determine the
protected trace.

That executable witness is not a complete algebraic-IOP honest-verifier
simulation and is not promoted into statistical, computational, or QROM zero
knowledge. Merkle integrity relies on SHA-256; any hiding additionally relies
on the entropy of sealed values, not collision resistance alone. The exact V17
bad-denominator distance also remains unresolved.

## 7. Judgment

For its exact receipt identity, V17 earns the label
`offline-theorem-qualified-candidate`: the relation, proof theorem, privacy
observer, linked verifier infrastructure, and every input script meet their
stated offline gates together.

The label is intentionally narrower than production deployment. BCHN evidence
is per-input `VerifyScript`, not transaction-level `CheckTransaction` or
`CheckTxInputs`; no live UTXO, maturity, relative-locktime, standardness,
mempool, mining, or broadcast result is claimed. V17 is also far above the
future sub-100,000-byte standard-relay target. Only the human user can declare
the named research end complete.
